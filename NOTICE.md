## Why some older material names a different account

The project was previously hosted under the account `UtkarshloneyaITG`, and was
transferred to `gamith24`. Two artifacts of that period still carry the former
name, and both are deliberately left alone:

- **Published releases v0.1.0 – v0.1.8** were distributed with a copyright notice
  naming the former account. Those files are already in circulation; their
  contents are a matter of record and are not rewritten here.
- **Git commit authorship** for commits made before the transfer names the former
  account. History is not rewritten, because the release tags point at those
  commits and rewriting would invalidate every published tag.

Neither is a competing ownership claim. They are a record of who published what,
when. Current authority rests with `gamith24`.

## What this file is, and is not

This is a statement of the project's position, recorded in the repository so it
is unambiguous to anyone reading it.

It is **not** a legal instrument. Transferring a repository does not by itself
transfer copyright — in most jurisdictions that requires a written assignment
between the parties. If `gamith24` and the former account are not the same
person, a signed assignment is what makes the position above enforceable, and
that document lives outside this repository. Nothing here is legal advice.
